## About Me

I'm currently in a combined Master's and Ph.D. program (2-year master's + 4-year Ph.D.) at the [School of Intelligent Science and Technology](https://is.nju.edu.cn/), **Nanjing University**, advised by [Prof. Yao Yao](https://yoyo000.github.io/).

Before that, I received my B.S. in Artificial Intelligence from **Xi'an Jiaotong University**, together with an honors degree from the Qian Xuesen Honors College.

My research interests include **spatial understanding**, **world models**, and **video generation**.
